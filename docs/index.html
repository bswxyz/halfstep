Every part, stated plainly.

01 — Frame

6061 alloy · hydroformed · 68° head angle

Welds you can see, geometry you can trust. Long wheelbase, low standover, no toe overlap.

02 — Motor

65 Nm mid-drive · assist to 25 km/h

Torque where the pedals are, so it feels like legs — not a launch.

03 — Battery

540 Wh · 0–100 % in 4 h · 2.9 kg

Slides out of the downtube in one click. Charge at your desk, not in the stairwell.

04 — Front wheel

700c × 47 mm · reflective sidewall

Wide enough for tram tracks and February. Puncture belt as standard, not as an option.

05 — Rear wheel

5-speed internal hub · carbon belt

Shift standing still at a red light. The belt never wants oil and never marks your trousers.

06 — Saddle + post

steel rails · 40 mm suspension post

The city has potholes. Your spine shouldn't have to read them out loud.
